How the score is produced

Each answer pair is converted into a similarity value: set overlap for multi-select interests, ordinal distance for ranked choices, and trait distance for the personality scale. Traits where difference is often beneficial are scored for complementarity instead of sameness. Dimension scores are combined with fixed weights, adjusted by a values-and-communication interaction term, and passed through a non-linear calibration curve. The same answers always produce the same score.
